# Project vision

Somewhere is a small, persistent coastal island shared by anonymous visitors across time. It is not a social network. There are no profiles, follower counts, leaderboards, streaks or chat.

The central feeling: *someone else once stood exactly here*. The island makes that feeling physical through places, weather, sound and preserved moments rather than through messages and metrics.

## Non-negotiables

1. The world must be beautiful enough to stand still in.
2. The automatic camera presents the island; it never demands manual operation.
3. Historical presence (Echoes) and live presence are never confused.
4. A visitor alone still has a complete experience.
5. Nothing is fabricated. If another person has not been here, the interface says so.
6. One island, composed with intent. No procedural sprawl.

## Success

A visitor sits on the bench, watches the water, and stays longer than they planned. Later, another visitor sits in the same place and understands that the first person was really there.
